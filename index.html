Arrive 30 minutes before your appointment time — arrive late and you may be refused admission. Each session runs on its own clock, so treat Session 1 as a closed box: finish it complete before it ends.

Take with you

  • Your international travel passport. Nothing else counts as ID — check the expiry date this week, not in November.
  • Your calculator, plus its cover, a keystroke card and loose batteries — all three are explicitly allowed in the room. For the BA II Plus you may also bring a small screwdriver to change them.
  • Water in a clear container with a lid, label removed.
  • Layers. Test-centre temperature is not negotiable and you're there five and a half hours.

Leave at home

  • Any watch at all — not just smart watches. Wristwatches, desk clocks and timers of every kind are banned. The on-screen timer is your only clock.
  • Phones, tablets, headphones, fitness trackers, anything Bluetooth.
  • Notes, textbooks, study guides, your own pens or paper.
  • Bags of any kind, food, and any drink that isn't water.
  • Jewellery beyond a wedding or engagement ring — also ornate hair clips, headbands, tie clips and cufflinks.

Calculator

Only two models: the TI BA II Plus (including Professional) and the HP 12C (including Platinum, Prestige and the anniversary editions). Manuals are barred, you cannot borrow one at the centre, and using an unapproved model voids your result — whether or not a proctor spots it on the day.

Set it up now, not in the exam room. On the BA II Plus:

  • 2ND · FORMAT · 9 · ENTER — floating decimals, so intermediate answers stop getting rounded.
  • 2ND · P/Y · 1 · ENTER — one payment per year. It ships set to 12 and quietly wrecks every TVM answer until you fix it.
  • Check you're in END mode, not BGN, unless the question is an annuity due.
  • 2ND · CLR TVM between problems — a leftover value from the last question is the most common self-inflicted wrong answer.
  • Practise the CF / NPV / IRR and ICONV worksheets until they're muscle memory. Fumbling those costs minutes you don't have.

Working the questions

  • 90 seconds per question is the budget CFA Institute itself gives: 90 questions in 135 minutes. Anything you're still fighting at two minutes is costing you an easier mark elsewhere.
  • There is no penalty for a wrong answer. Never leave one blank — three choices means a pure guess is a one-in-three shot, and every question carries equal weight.
  • Read the qualifier. Questions turn on most likely, least likely and best described, and that word is often the whole question.
  • The exam does not use negatively framed items, so you won't be caught by a hidden "not". Read for the qualifier instead.
  • Answer what's asked, in the units asked. Percentage or decimal, annual or periodic, thousands or millions — wrong units are a right answer scored wrong.

Afterwards

Results land 5 to 7 weeks later: pass or fail, plus a performance summary by topic area. CFA Institute does not publish the minimum passing score, so no percentage you hit in practice is a guarantee — which is exactly why the trend across your mocks matters more than any single one.
